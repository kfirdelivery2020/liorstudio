CourierFinance

Track a courier's income and expenses, and see what you really earn.

Coming to Google Play Privacy Policy

What it does

Income and expenses

Record income and expenses by category and business, including adding several entries or several dates at once.

Quick cash

Your most common business and amount combinations from your history, added in one tap in the middle of a delivery day.

Vehicles and mileage

Track a car, scooter or bike: mileage, fuel, repairs, and license and insurance reminders.

Statistics

Daily and monthly net profit, comparison with the previous month, a breakdown by area, and cost per km.

Debts and reminders

What you owe and what you are owed, with a reminder for a payment that has not been marked as paid.

Backup and building codes

Export and import a backup, and save building entry codes organized by street.

Permissions and privacy

The app asks for notification permission so it can send reminders that it creates itself, such as a reminder about an unconfirmed payment. It does not read notifications from other apps.

Full details about stored information and data deletion are in the Privacy Policy and the account deletion page.
