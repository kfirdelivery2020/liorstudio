CourierInfo

Smart info for couriers. Shows the distance in km of deliveries across different delivery apps, so you can tell right away if a delivery is worth taking.

What it does

Real-time profitability

A floating window over the delivery app shows the offer's details, such as price and distance, when it appears.

History and statistics

Keeps your deliveries and shows summaries, so you can see how your days actually look.

Building codes

One place to save building entry codes, organized by street.

How it works

  1. Install the app and sign in.
  2. Grant the permissions it needs, explained below.
  3. When a delivery offer appears in a supported app, CourierInfo reads the information shown on screen and displays its profitability in a floating window.

Permissions and privacy

The app asks for three permissions, each needed for what it does:

  • Accessibility service: reads what is shown on screen in supported delivery apps to calculate profitability. It does not perform clicks, type text, modify content on the device or collect passwords.
  • Notification access: to identify deliveries and relevant alerts from supported apps.
  • Overlay (draw over other apps): to show the information in real time over other apps.

The app does not collect GPS location, passwords, private messages, phone numbers or credit card details, and does not sell personal information. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Why does the app need an accessibility service?

To read the information shown on screen in supported delivery apps and calculate profitability for you. The service does not perform clicks, type text, modify content on the device or collect passwords.

What data does the app collect?

Username, email address, user ID, delivery data such as price, distance and source app, settings and preferences, subscription and permission data, and basic usage data needed to operate the app. The full list is in the Privacy Policy.

What does the app not collect?

GPS location, passwords, private messages, phone numbers and credit card details.

Is the data sold or shared with delivery companies?

No. The app does not sell personal information to third parties and does not share personal information with delivery companies.

How do I delete my data?

Through the options available in the app. You can also clear local data through the app or through the device settings.
