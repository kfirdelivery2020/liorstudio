K Delivery

Our own delivery service: private deliveries in Harish and the surrounding area.

Coming to Google Play Privacy Policy

What it does

Order a delivery

Choose a delivery type and an address, see the delivery price and confirm your order.

My orders

All your orders in one place, with their status, and the option to cancel an order.

Saved addresses

Save addresses like home and work, including a building code and notes for the courier, and pick a default address.

Price list

Prices by town, in Harish and the surrounding area, before you order.

Coupons

Coupons waiting for you, with an automatic suggestion when one fits your order.

Opening hours

See when you can order. When we are closed you can still send an order, and it is handled when we open.

How it works

  1. Sign up with an email or a Google account.
  2. Choose a delivery type and an address.
  3. Confirm the order and follow it in "My orders".

Privacy
