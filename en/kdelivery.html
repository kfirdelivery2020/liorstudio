K Delivery

Our own delivery service: private deliveries in Harish and the surrounding area.

Screenshots

What it does

Order a delivery

Choose a delivery type and an address, see the delivery price and confirm your order.

My orders

All your orders in one place, with their status, and the option to cancel an order.

Saved addresses

Save addresses like home and work, including a building code and notes for the courier, and pick a default address.

Price list

Prices by town, in Harish and the surrounding area, before you order.

Coupons

Coupons waiting for you, with an automatic suggestion when one fits your order.

Opening hours

See when you can order. When we are closed you can still send an order, and it is handled when we open.

How it works

  1. Sign up with an email or a Google account.
  2. Choose a delivery type and an address.
  3. Confirm the order and follow it in "My orders".

Privacy

Full details about stored information and data deletion are in the Privacy Policy. For questions, use the contact page.

FAQ

How do I sign up?

With an email and password, or with a Google account.

Which areas do you serve?

Harish and the surrounding area. The in-app price list shows prices by town.

What happens when you are closed?

You can still send an order and it is handled when we open. Opening hours are shown in the app.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, from the "My orders" screen. Cancelling an order cannot be undone.

Where can I read about privacy?

The details are in the Privacy Policy, and you can reach us through the contact page.
