About

Lior Studio is an independent software studio based in Israel. We design, build and maintain Android and iOS apps.

Lior Studio was founded to build simple, reliable tools for people and businesses: couriers, restaurants and everyday users.

Who is behind Lior Studio

Lior Studio is run by Kfir Yaakobi from Harish. We build apps for couriers and everyday users, and run a private delivery service in Harish and the surrounding area.
