Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy describes how Lior Studio ("we", "us") handles information in this website and in our mobile apps. Some apps have their own policy, linked from the apps section of the home page; where an app policy exists, it applies to that app.

In short

This website is static and has no accounts, forms, analytics or advertising cookies. Each app collects only the information it needs to work, and we do not sell personal information. You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information by email. Our apps are not directed to children under 13. This summary does not replace the full text below.

This website

This website is static. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising cookies. We do not collect personal information through it, other than what you choose to send us by email.

The website does not set cookies. To remember your dark/light mode choice and accessibility menu settings, your browser stores small values in the device local storage (localStorage). These values stay on your device and are not sent to us or to any third party.

Our apps

Each app collects only the information it needs to work. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with third parties except service providers that process it on our behalf (for example, hosting and authentication), and where the law requires it.

Alertly asks for Android notification access so it can read notifications on the device and match them against rules the user sets. This matching happens on the device.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing us. Apps that create accounts let you request account deletion.

Security and children

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information. Our apps are not directed to children under 13.
