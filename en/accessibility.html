Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 2026

Lior Studio considers it important that this website be accessible to all users, including people with disabilities, and works to improve its accessibility on an ongoing basis.

What we did

  • Every page has an accessibility button that lets you enlarge or reduce text, turn on high contrast, highlight links, switch to a readable font and stop motion. The settings are stored on your device only.
  • The site can be navigated with a keyboard, and the current position is clearly marked. Every page starts with a "Skip to content" link.
  • The site is built with headings and semantic elements, and supports Hebrew (right to left) and English.
  • There is a dark mode and a light mode, and the site is adapted to phone screens.
  • The site respects the device's reduced-motion setting.

Standard and limitations

We aim to meet the Israeli Standard SI 5568 at level AA (based on WCAG 2.0), as far as possible. The site has not yet been reviewed by a certified external party, some parts may not be fully accessible, and we continue to improve it.
