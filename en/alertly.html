Alertly

Filters your notifications by keywords and shows only what matters to you. Made for everyone.

Coming to Google Play Privacy Policy

What it does

Words, names and phrases

Choose what to look for, and the app alerts you when it appears in a message.

You choose the apps

Only the apps you select are read. An app you did not select is not read at all.

Precise filtering

Add words you do not want to see, and limit alerts to specific people or groups.

No flooding

Set a gap between alerts so many messages from one chat do not flood you. You can also snooze an alert for an hour, 3 hours or a week.

Your own sound

Pick a sound and get an alert from Alertly itself, so you recognize it right away.

Test before you rely on it

A test screen shows whether a given message would match a rule you created.

How it works

  1. Choose which apps to watch.
  2. Choose what to look for: a word, a name or a phrase that matters to you.
  3. When a message contains it, you get an alert from us, with the sound you chose.

Notification access and privacy

To work, the app needs notification access on the device. You grant it yourself in the phone's settings, and you can revoke it at any time.

  • Message content stays on your device. It is not sent anywhere and is not used for advertising.
  • The app is read-only. It does not send messages, reply or touch other apps.
  • Message content is not kept in permanent storage by default. Only the word that matched is saved.
  • The app is free and shows Google ads, inside the app only.

More details are in the Privacy Policy.
